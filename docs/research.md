# Target research

Checked 14 November 2026. Family: lead-qualification. No existing build in the registry used this family. Distinct from contact storage, routing a lead to a rep and aggregate product analytics.

MadKudu's pricing address redirects to [HG Insights](https://www.madkudu.com/pricing), with no standalone public amount. The page therefore quotes no vendor price. [Vendr's MadKudu profile](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/madkudu?contractType=renewal&requestType=priceEstimate) reports a median annual contract of US$33,644 from 40 purchases. This is third-party purchase evidence, not a current vendor offer or a verified NZ/AU customer invoice. The cached search result was available; direct access to Vendr's base URL returned 403.

Scores in order: annual bill 3, data-shaped 5, our buyers 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 5, import path 4. Total 22/30. Local customer and CRM-context files supplied no MadKudu purchase evidence. Buyer fit is an inference; search volume was not measured. [G2 alternatives](https://www.g2.com/products/madkudu/competitors/alternatives) shows comparison demand and overlapping vendors, not measured traffic for this site.

[Vendor model guide](https://help.hginsights.com/docs/what-are-the-different-models) distinguishes fit and engagement. [Bulk Scorer](https://help.hginsights.com/docs/madkudu-bulk-scorer-2) and [field specifications](https://help.hginsights.com/docs/madkudu-field-names-and-specifications) establish the contact export route. Local weighted rules are a deliberately different system, not reverse engineered vendor predictions. Proprietary enrichment and contact discovery remain outside scope.

Rejected candidates: Ziflow 15/30 (1,2,2,3,2,5), because the documented base bill is below the threshold and visual proofing is central; Supercede 14/30 (1,4,1,2,3,3), because no five-figure annual bill was verified and the trading network is outside scope.
